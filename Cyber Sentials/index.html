01 // MODERN ATTACK VECTORS

Deconstructing common digital traps.

Cybercriminals rely on urgency, authority deception, and human psychology far more than sophisticated malware. Learn their methods before encountering them.

CASE 01

UPI & QR Code Scams

Scammers list items on marketplaces (OLX, Facebook) or offer refunds, sending a QR code with the claim: "Scan this QR code to receive your advance payment."

⚠️ Warning Signs
  • Asking you to enter your UPI PIN to "receive" funds
  • Fake payment screenshot showing money debited from buyer
🛡️ Prevention Rule
Golden Rule: UPI PIN is ONLY entered when deducting money from your account, never to receive money.
CASE 02

Digital Arrest Scams

Victims receive video calls from individuals in police uniforms claiming a parcel with narcotics or money laundering has been intercepted under their Aadhaar.

⚠️ Warning Signs
  • Threat of "Digital Arrest" over Skype or WhatsApp call
  • Demand to transfer money into an "escrow account" for verification
🛡️ Prevention Rule
Legal Fact: "Digital Arrest" does not exist under Indian law. Law enforcement never conducts trials via video calls.
CASE 03

WhatsApp Account Hijacking

An acquaintance or relative asks you: "I accidentally sent an OTP to your phone, please forward it to me." Forwarding it immediately surrenders your WhatsApp account.

⚠️ Warning Signs
  • Sudden emergency messages asking for 6-digit codes
  • Unknown phone login prompt appearing on your screen
🛡️ Prevention Rule
Action: Never share verification codes. Immediately enable Two-Step Verification in WhatsApp settings.
CASE 04

Remote Access & Screen Sharing

Posing as customer care or courier support, scammers ask you to install apps like AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or RustDesk to "help verify your payment" or "complete KYC".

⚠️ Warning Signs
  • Caller requesting a 9-digit code from AnyDesk/QuickSupport
  • Instruction to open banking app while on the call
🛡️ Prevention Rule
Strict Defense: Legitimate customer service never demands screen-sharing apps to process refunds or KYC.
CASE 05

Fake Electricity Bill Scams

An urgent SMS arrives: "Dear Consumer, your electricity power will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM due to unpaid bill. Call officer at 98xxxxxxx immediately."

⚠️ Warning Signs
  • Sent from personal 10-digit mobile numbers, not official sender IDs
  • Directing you to call a personal phone or install an APK file
🛡️ Prevention Rule
Check First: Check pending dues exclusively through your official electricity distribution portal or app.
CASE 06

Phishing & Fake Links

Links imitating banks, courier delivery (India Post / BlueDart), or income tax portals sent through SMS/email asking you to update PAN, pay ₹10 fees, or claim prizes.

⚠️ Warning Signs
  • Misspelled domain names (e.g., sbi-login-kyc.com, indiapost-track.xyz)
  • Shortened bit.ly or tinyurl links requiring immediate credentials
🛡️ Prevention Rule
Inspect URLs: Always verify domain endings. Authentic government sites strictly end with .gov.in or .nic.in.
02 // CRITICAL PROTOCOL

Emergency response roadmap.

If you suspect fraud or an unauthorized transaction has occurred, follow these exact 5 steps in chronological order. Speed is paramount.

01

Immediately Sever All Communication

Hang up the phone call, disconnect video chats, and cease texting the suspected scammer. Do not explain or confront them. If you installed remote software (AnyDesk, TeamViewer), turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data immediately to cut their connection.

Within Minutes
02

Contact Bank or Payment Provider Instantly

Call your bank's 24/7 emergency fraud helpline or use your mobile banking application to freeze your debit/credit card, block net banking access, and stop UPI services. Request an official reference/complaint ticket number.

Window: < 1 Hour
03

Call 1930 for Financial Cyber Fraud

Dial 1930 (National Cyber Crime Reporting Helpline). State your bank name, account number, transaction UTR number, and time of transaction. Calling within 2 hours enables authorities to trigger inter-bank lien holds on fraudulent recipient accounts.

Helpline: 1930
04

Register Official FIR on cybercrime.gov.in

Log on to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in). Lodge a formal complaint under "Report Cyber Fraud". Keep a digital copy of your Acknowledgement Slip for bank follow-ups.

Statutory Portal
05

Preserve Critical Evidentiary Trail

Do not delete chat histories, SMS alerts, call logs, or transaction receipts. Capture clean screenshots showing timestamps, suspect phone numbers, and bank UTR numbers. Use our Evidence Checklist below to ensure nothing is missed.

Preservation

05 // INVESTIGATION READINESS

Critical evidence collection checklist.

Law enforcement and banks require concrete digital forensic records to freeze funds and track suspects. Check off each item as you gather it.

Evidence Artifacts Collected: 0 of 7 TAP TO TOGGLE

Transaction Screenshots

Debit confirmation screen showing date, time, debited account, and recipient title.

UTR / Transaction ID Reference

The exact 12-digit Unique Transaction Reference (UTR) provided by your bank statement.

Bank SMS Notifications

Raw text messages with sender alphanumeric headers (e.g. VM-HDFCBK) informing of the debit.

Suspect Phone Numbers & Handles

Full 10-digit mobile number, WhatsApp profile details, Skype ID, or social media handles.

Suspicious Links & Domain URLs

The complete URL string clicked or shared via SMS (do not re-open the link).

Complete Chat Histories

Full exported conversation logs on WhatsApp, Telegram, or email without deletion.

Email Headers & Attached Files

If contacted via email, export the original .eml file or raw headers showing the originating IP address.

06 // VERIFIED DIRECTORY

Official statutory portals and helplines.

Always report directly through recognized Government of India civic portals. Never engage unverified intermediaries claiming to recover lost funds for an upfront fee.

PRIMARY REPORTING

cybercrime.gov.in

The Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. Lodge formal complaints for financial fraud and cyber offenses against women and children.

Visit Official Portal
TELECOM SECURITY

sancharsaathi.gov.in

Department of Telecommunications initiative enabling citizens to block lost or stolen mobile handsets (CEIR), report fraudulent calls (Chakshu), and check connections registered under your ID.

Visit Sanchar Saathi
IMMEDIATE CALL

Helpline 1930

The Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting and Management System helpline. Direct link to national payment banks, gateways, and cyber police units for real-time fund freezing.

Call 1930 (Toll-Free)
